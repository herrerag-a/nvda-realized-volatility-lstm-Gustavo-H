# nvda-realized-volatility-lstm-Gustavo-H
Developed a multivariate LSTM model to forecast next-day NVIDIA realized volatility using historical NVDA volatility and the VIX. Applied chronological train/validation/test splitting, log transformations, feature standardization, early stopping, and out-of-sample evaluation using MAE and RMSE. QQQ and TSM were also tested. 
The LSTM models were able to capture the persistence and general direction of NVIDIA’s realized volatility, but they were less effective at predicting the magnitude of sudden volatility spikes. Among the tested specifications, the best-performing model was a two-layer multivariate LSTM using NVDA realized volatility and VIX, with 8 neurons per layer and a 15-day sequence length. It achieved a test MAE of 0.4651 and RMSE of 0.7348.
Adding additional variables such as QQQ and TSM did not improve performance, suggesting that increasing model complexity does not necessarily lead to better volatility forecasts. The results indicate that the LSTM captures volatility persistence reasonably well, while unexpected market shocks remain difficult to forecast from historical information alone.
